## Exact removal projection recovery contract

Internal authenticated POST `repairTradeProjection` may include `removedScheduleIds:["YYMMDD-NNN-NN"]` only after the caller has reconciled the original completed deletion and current source state. This is an app projection repair, not a repeat sheet deletion. Never submit a broad keep-set or infer all missing rows should be deleted. The server rejects malformed/cross-trade/duplicate IDs, remote authority failure, checkout-started/returned trades, and IDs currently present in the sheet (rechecked under structural lock). Existing taken-quantity protection remains in the projection worker. Completion still requires a fresh app/DB readback; queued does not mean completed.

Review reproduced an additional queue race: a deleted highest suffix can be reused by a later addition, while an older queued tombstone persists. The fix cancels older removal grants on readdition, rechecks the current queue revision and actual GAS rows immediately before exclusion, and takes a short target-trade claim during external HTTP. Global ScriptLock is released before HTTP. Shared mutation guards plus legacy registration-merge queuing prevent additions/checkout from crossing this final check/apply boundary; unrelated trades remain available. Recovery never replays a sheet deletion.

An internal `repairTradeProjection` request with `statusOnly:true` reads queue/source/claim/trigger state without queuing, deleting, rescheduling or making remote business calls. Tokens, credentials and unrelated properties are never returned. Failed worker attempts retain their stage for diagnosis. This was added when a successful enqueue did not converge; read-only diagnostics must precede any further recovery decision.
