# Live registration evidence IDs and unresolved aliases

## Confirmed demand versus unknown identity

When staff accepted the exact current demand, preserve the complete approved raw cart through the validated no-send registration route. Missing catalog membership, stock or price does not erase that approval. Reconcile the exact RQ/trade, register once, verify every approved name/quantity/period, and retain catalog/supply/pending-price follow-up separately. Do not invent a price, supply promise or equipment substitution. An included component already expanded from the set can remain unchanged.

Ask one focused question only when the actual accepted equipment identity remains materially ambiguous after conversation and authoritative master lookup. A missing autocomplete entry alone is not ambiguity. Known approved demand must not wait for an unrelated unresolved choice.

## Evidence-ID boundary

`commit-registration-live` binds selected customer and staff IDs exclusively to the immutable Kakao snapshot. Use the relevant Kakao confirmation, not an unrelated bubble or a fabricated ID. For Slack-only authority use the documented snapshot/strict cross-channel route, carrying the real Slack event ID. Preserve one operation UUID and reconcile uncertain/partial results before any further write. Neither registration nor customer sending is proved by an agent response alone.
